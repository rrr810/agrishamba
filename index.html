How it works

Five steps from listing to delivery

  1. Register

    Create a free account as a farmer, buyer, supplier or service provider.

  2. List or Find

    Sellers publish listings with photos, price and location. Buyers search and filter.

  3. Connect

    Message the seller, agree on quantity, grading and delivery terms.

  4. Pay

    Checkout with M-Pesa or card. Funds are confirmed before dispatch.

  5. Delivery

    Track the order through dispatch and delivery, then rate the transaction.
