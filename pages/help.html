Help Center & FAQ

Answers to common questions about buying produce, farmer listings, rider deliveries, and M-Pesa payments.

Browse the Marketplace, filter by category or your county, select items, and add them to your cart. Proceed to checkout, enter your delivery address in Kenya, and pay securely via M-Pesa or card.
When you pay, funds are held securely in escrow until you inspect and confirm receipt of your produce. Once you click "Yes, I received it", funds are released to the farmer and delivery rider. If there's an issue, you can tap "Dispute" to halt payout and request support.
Sign up or log in as a Farmer or Supplier, go to Sell a Product, upload photos of your produce, set your price per unit (e.g. KES 4,200 per 90kg bag), specify available stock and location. Your listing is immediately visible to buyers across Kenya.
Register with a Rider / Transport account. Open your Rider Dashboard to view available delivery jobs in your county. Each job displays the pickup farm location, buyer drop-off location, cargo weight, and your delivery payout.
We support M-Pesa STK Push (instant prompt on your Safaricom phone) and Debit/Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard) processed securely through Paystack.
Our Farm Cost Calculator allows you to select your crop, acreage, and input costs (seeds, fertilizer, labor, irrigation). It generates projected yields, expected gross revenue, total production cost, and your break-even selling price before you plant.
