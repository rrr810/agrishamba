About SokoShamba

Building the digital infrastructure Kenyan agriculture runs on.

Why we exist

Kenyan farmers routinely sell below the value of their produce because the path to buyers runs through several intermediaries, and buyers struggle to find consistent, traceable supply. Inputs, transport and professional services are equally fragmented — often arranged over phone calls and word of mouth.

SokoShamba brings those interactions into one platform: a marketplace where produce, inputs, livestock and equipment are listed with clear prices and locations; dashboards that show sellers what is actually happening in their business; and planning tools that help farmers decide before they spend.

What the platform covers

🛒

Marketplace

Listings across cereals, horticulture, livestock, dairy, poultry, seeds, fertilizer, feed and equipment.

📊

Business tools

Role-specific dashboards for farmers, buyers, suppliers and service providers.

🧮

Planning

Cost calculators, break-even analysis and reference market prices.

🚚

Services

Transport, storage, machinery, labour, veterinary, irrigation and consulting.

Build status

ℹ️
This deployment is the frontend application in demo mode. All products, orders, prices and user accounts shown are clearly-labelled sample data held in your browser. Authentication, database, storage, payments and automation are architected and ready to connect to Supabase, Paystack and an email/automation provider. Nothing on this site represents a real business or a real transaction.

Principles

  • ✅ Honest data. Demo content is labelled as demo content — never presented as real traction.
  • ✅ Security first. No secret keys in the browser; sensitive operations run server-side.
  • ✅ Built for mobile. Most Kenyan farmers reach the internet on a phone, so mobile is the default.
  • ✅ Low friction. Fast pages, no heavy frameworks, works on modest connections.
