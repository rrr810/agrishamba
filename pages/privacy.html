Privacy policy

Draft policy for the SokoShamba demo build.

⚠️
Template document. This is a placeholder for the demo build and is not legal advice. Have a qualified advisor prepare the final policy in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 before launch.

1. Data we collect

Account details (name, email, phone, county), listing content you publish, orders you place or receive, messages sent through the platform, and technical data such as browser type and device.

2. Demo mode storage

In this demo build no data leaves your device. Cart contents, demo session, saved products, listings and preferences are held in your browser's localStorage and can be cleared at any time from Settings → Account → Reset demo data. We never store passwords or card details there.

3. How we use data

To operate the marketplace, process orders and payments, provide support, prevent fraud, and improve the product. We do not sell personal data.

4. Payments

Payment processing will be handled by a licensed provider (Paystack and mobile money partners). Card data is entered on the provider's secure checkout and is never processed or stored by SokoShamba.

5. Sharing

When you transact, the counterparty sees the details necessary to fulfil the order — name, phone number and delivery location. Service providers such as hosting, payments and email operate under contract.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and object to certain processing. Contact support@sokoshamba.example.

7. Security

Access to production data is restricted using database row level security, encrypted connections and least-privilege service credentials that are never exposed to the browser.

8. Changes

We will publish any material change to this policy on this page with a revised effective date.
